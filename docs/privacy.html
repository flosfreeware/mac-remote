Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Overview

Mac Remote does not collect, sell, or share personal data. We do not operate accounts, analytics, or advertising.

How the app works

The iPhone app communicates directly with the Mac you choose to pair, using your local connection. Remote-control commands and connection diagnostics are sent only between those paired devices; they are not sent to us or to any third party.

Software updates

If you leave automatic updates enabled, the Mac app checks for available updates when it starts and about every 12 hours. This request goes to our GitHub Pages update file. Like any web request, GitHub receives technical connection information such as your IP address and request metadata. The update check does not send your pairing credentials, remote-control activity, diagnostics data, or personal content.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you choose to scan a pairing QR code. Camera images are not stored or uploaded.
  • Local Network: used to find and communicate with your paired Mac.
  • Motion: used to control the pointer when you enable motion control.

Local storage

Pairing credentials and app preferences are stored locally on your devices. Any diagnostics results are stored locally on your Mac. You can remove the pairing and reset the app from its settings.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy, please open an issue on the Mac Remote GitHub repository.
